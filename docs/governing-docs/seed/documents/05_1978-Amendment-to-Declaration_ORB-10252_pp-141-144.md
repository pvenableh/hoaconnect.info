RESOLUTION AMENDING DECLARATION OF CONDOMINIUM

OF

LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC.

We, SIGMOND KAUFMAN, President; ESTELLE VINCENT, Secretary; SYLVIA LIEMAN, Vice President and SOHPIE LEVINE, Treasurer, the newly elected officers of LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC., a Florida corporation, do hereby certify that the following is a true and correct copy of the Resolution duly adopted by the Board of Directors of said corporation and the entire Condominium Association' at a meeting personally attended by a quorum of the entire Condominium Association membership, which meeting was duly called in conformity with the By-Laws of said corporation and was held at 1033 Lenox Avenue, at 7:30 p.m. on October 17, 1978.

RESOLVED, that the officers of this corporation and the entire Condominium Association at the meeting stated above have adopted in accordance with the By-Laws of said corporation the following amendments to the Declaration of Condominium of LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC., a condominium in Dade County, Florida, recorded in Official Records Book 9640, Page 258, of the Public Records of Dade County, Florida, as follows:

*[in the paragraph above, the words "and the entire" and "Association at the" are typed over an earlier strike and are hard to read]*

1\. Exhibit "A" of the Declaration of Condominium is amended by changing the parking space numbers as indicated on the attached Exhibit "A", Survey & Site Plan. Said Exhibit shall become the amended survey, removing the original survey from said Declaration.

2\. Paragraph 4(d), Page 3, which reads "**Automobile Parking.** The use of automobile parking areas for automobile parking, subject to the provisions hereof". Said paragraph shall be amended by adding to it:

(i) no automobile parking space may be used for any purpose other than the parking of automobiles which are in operating condition. No parking space shall be used by any person other than an occupant of the condominium who is in actual residence or who is a guest or visitor of a unit owner and upon the premises for said purpose. Unit owner's shall have an identification sticker displayed clearly "Permit Parking". Only one car per unit owner in accordance with the designation on amended Exhibit "A", will be able to park on the premises. Guests or visitors driving automobiles shall also be required to display identification. Any car without identification will be towed away without notice and redeemed at the expense of the car owner who will be required to pay for said towing.

*[handwritten in the margin: "13.00"]*

{O.R. 10252, p. 142}

(ii) no washing of cars with hoses on the premises and no major repair work will be permitted.

(iii) there shall be no parking under the overhang of the building. Every car must be parked within its designated lines of its parking slot, otherwise the car will be towed away.

3\. Declaration of Condominium #12(.3)(a)(1), shall be amended to read in the following manner: "**Sale.** An apartment owner intending to make a bona fide sale of his apartment or any interest therein shall give to the Association notice, in writing, of such intention, together with the name and address of the intended purchaser and such other information concerning the intended purchaser as the Association may reasonably require before the unit to be sold is advertised for sale. The notice to the Association may include a demand by the apartment owner that the Association furnish a purchaser if the proposed purchaser is not approved; and if such demand is made, the notice shall be accompanied by an executed copy of the proposed contract to sell".

4\. Declaration of Condominium #12(.3)(b) **Certificate of Approval.** (1) "**Sale.** Shall be amended to read the following: If the proposed transaction is a sale, the purchaser must come together with the seller before the Board of Directors for their approval. Within fifteen (15) days after receipt of such notice and information to the Association, the appearance by the purchaser and seller before the Board of Directors must be made. Within sixty (60) days after notice and information to the Association of the sale, including the appearance before the Board, the transaction shall be approved or non-approved and it shall be so stated in writing, executed by the President or Secretary of the Association, a Certificate of Approval or Disapproval, in recordable form, and shall be delivered to the seller and recorded in the Public Records of Dade County".

5\. Declaration of Condominium #12(.1) **Transfers Subject to Approval.** (b) **Lease.** Shall be amended by being omitted in its entirety from this subsection, and subsection (c) below the omitted **Lease** amendment shall be amended to become subsection (b), entitled **Gift or Inheritance.**

6\. Declaration of Condominium #12(.2)(a) **Lease.** Shall be amended to read the following: No leasing or renting of any unit shall be permitted whatsoever for any period of time no matter how short. No person inheriting the apartment through death of owner can lease or rent the apartment. If said surviving heir does not choose to live in the apartment, said Estate or person shall pay normal maintenance or must sell the unit. Exception to a next of kin occupying the unit.

{O.R. 10252, p. 143}

7\. Declaration of Condominium #11 shall be amended to read the following: "**Residential Use Restrictions Applicable to Private Dwellings.** Each PRIVATE DWELLING is hereby restricted to residential use by the owner or owners thereof. Each unit is permitted one guest for one month of the year. Each additional stay or guest will be subject to an additional contribution to monthly maintenance. No owner or owners of any PRIVATE DWELLING shall permit use of same for transient, hotel or commercial purposes. No children under eighteen (18) years of age are permitted to be permanent household members. All units consist of one bedroom apartments designed for two persons. Exceptions to said rule are Apartment 304 for one child and Apartment 310 for one extra adult. These exceptions are as of the date of filing the within Amendment.

## Transcriber's notes

- The scan is a dark photocopy. Every word above was read from the page image; nothing is supplied from another document.  
- Paragraph 7 opens a quotation mark before "Residential Use Restrictions" and never closes it. Paragraph 4 opens one before "Sale." and closes it after "Dade County".  
- "SOHPIE LEVINE" is typed that way; the signature reads Sophie Levine. "Unit owner's shall have" and "Association'" (a stray apostrophe) are as typed.  
- The opening certificate names four officers. The acknowledgment names only the President and Secretary.  
- Run-in headings shown in bold are underlined in the original.  
- Paragraph numbers 3 to 6 cite the Declaration as "#12(.3)(a)(1)" and similar; that punctuation is as typed.
