AMENDMENT TO DECLARATION OF CONDOMINIUM OF

LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC.

LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC. is a non-profit Florida corporation, the Declaration of Condominium of which is recorded in Official Records Book 9640, at page 258, of the Public Records of Dade County, Florida.

Lots 9 and 10, Block 119, LENOX MANOR of the Miami Ocean View Company's Subdivision. LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC. is a condominium at 1033 Lenox Avenue, Miami Beach, Florida 33139, Dade County.

Attached is new SURVEY AND SITE PLAN including revised reserved parking spaces. EXHIBIT "A". Each unit of the condominium has it's own parking space, there are no guest or second car slots available.

A unit owner not owning a car, may give their spot to another unit owner by written permission filed with the Board of Directors.

This is the second amendment to the Declaration of Condominium of Lenox Plaza Association, Inc. of "EXHIBIT "A".

The City of Miami Beach has requested a new-up to date SURVEY AND SITE PLAN, therefore, this amendment. New document dated September 20, 1995.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the undersigned President and Secretary have executed this instrument amending the Declaration of Condominium thereof on this 3 day of Nov, 1995.

## Transcriber's notes

- The President and Secretary are not named in type anywhere in the instrument, and their signatures are not transcribed as names.  
- The notary's stamp names Josephine Rubin. The signature on the President's line and the notary's signature look alike in the scan. Josephine Rubin signed the 1994 amendment as President.  
- Typing kept as in the original: "has it's own parking space", "new-up to date", "of "EXHIBIT "A".", "theCounty", and no period after "aforesaid".  
- Several phrases are printed in heavier type in the original ("EXHIBIT "A"", "guest or second car slots available", "not owning a car, may give their spot to another unit owner by written permission filed with the Board of Directors"). The emphasis is not reproduced.  
- A handwritten mark, apparently "15.00", appears in the lower margin of page 3006.
