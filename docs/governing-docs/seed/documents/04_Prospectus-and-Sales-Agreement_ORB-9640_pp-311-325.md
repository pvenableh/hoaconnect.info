PROSPECTUS OF LENOX PLAZA CONDOMINIUM, INC.

I.

a) Condominium shall be known as Lenox Plaza Condominium, Inc., located at 1033 Lenox Avenue, Miami Beach,Florida.

b) Maximum number of units using the common facilities shall be 28.

c) The survey showing the number of units and number of bedrooms and bathrooms of each unit is attached to the Declaration of Condominium and designated Exhibit "A".

d) The copy of the survey and site plan showing all of the recreational facilities are contained in the Declaration of Condominium designated as one of the Exhibits.

e) It is estimated that the latest date for completion shall be June 1, 1977.

II.

A) A description of the recreational and commonly used facilities is as follows:

a) The Recreation Room shall be 22 ft. by 29 ft. 4 inches, which equals 645.26 square feet. The Recreation Room will accommodate 43 persons and will be located in the area of the entrance to the building.

b) The developer represents that the minimum amount of money that the developer will spend for the recreation areas shall be $750.00, and the maximum amount shall be $1,000.00.

c) The estimated date when each room will be available for use by the unit owner is June 1, 1977.

III.

The parking area is located on Lots 9 and 10 of Block 119 of LENOX MANOR of the Miami Ocean View Company's Subdivision, according to the Plat thereof, as recorded in Plat Book 7, at Page 15, of the Public Records of Dade County, Florida.

IV.

The utility services for sewage, waste disposal, water supply and storm drainage shall be provided by the City of Miami Beach. Electrical services shall be provided by Florida Power & Light Company and telephone services by Southern Bell Telephone Company, and gas services by Peoples Gas Systems, Inc. It is also possible to have private garbage removal other than by the City of Miami Beach.

V.

The management of the Corporation, including maintenance and operation of the condominium shall be by the unit owners through the condominium association.

{O.R. 9640, p. 312}

VI.

The apportionment of common expenses and ownership of the common elements have, and shall be, determined on an equal payment basis per apartment, as set forth in the Declaration of Condominium.

VII.

The estimated operating budget for the condominium and the schedule of unit owner's expenses is attached hereto and marked Exhibit "C".

VIII.

The estimated closing expenses to be paid by the buyer shall be 1% of the purchase price of the condominium unit, and a title insurance policy shall be furnished to the buyer at the expense of the developer.

IX.

The developer is 1033 Lenox Corp., a Florida corporation, and its principal officer is Jack Sens, who is directing the creation and sale of the condominium. Mr. Sens has had extensive experience in the construction and sale of many condominiums in the City of Miami Beach.

Oral Representations cannot be relied upon as correctly stating the representations of the developer. For correct representations, reference should be made to this prospectus (offering circular) and its exhibits.

The condominium herein is not part of a phase project subject to the provisions of Section 711.64, Florida Statutes.

There are no restrictions on the use of the condominium parcel in conjunction with children and/or pets, it being the philosophy of the developer that condominium ownership should be akin to the ownership of a private home without unnecessary, unwarranted and cumbersome restrictions.

b) Proposed Articles of Incorporation of Condominium Association (See proposed By-Laws of Condominium Association);

## Transcriber's notes

**Wording kept as it appears in the original**

- The Prospectus calls the condominium "Lenox Plaza Condominium, Inc." The Declaration and Articles use "Lenox Plaza Association, Inc."  
- Prospectus II has an item A) and no item B).  
- Prospectus IX: the sentence "Important matters to be considered in acquiring a condominium unit:" is followed directly by the schedule of exhibits.  
- Sales Agreement, second recital: "an Apartment Building consisting of 12 units".  
- Sales Agreement, closing paragraph: "NO PART OF SUCH FINDS".  
- Missing spaces after commas ("Miami Beach,Florida", "prospectus,to wit", "municipality,shall", "EXERCISED,HOWEVER", "EUGENE J.WEISS") are as typed.

**Readings settled by the transcriber**

- Blanks in the form are shown as underscores; their lengths are not to scale.  
- Page 313: the fraction in "3½%" is small in the scan and is read from the matching "3.5%" in clause 2 of the Sales Agreement.  
- Page 317: the dimension "23' X 14'" is printed between the Dining and Living labels and is entered in the Living column.  
- Floor plan room labels are printed in small capitals; they are given here in ordinary capitalization. The drawings themselves are not reproduced.  
- Sales Agreement clause 2: the percentage in "One (1%) per cent" is partly filled in on the scan and is read from Prospectus VIII and the brochure.
