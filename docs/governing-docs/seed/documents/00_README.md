## Conventions

- **Page markers.** `{O.R. 9640, p. 259}` on its own line marks the start of that recorded page. A marker can fall in the middle of a sentence, because the original page ends there.
- **Brackets.** Italic text in square brackets, `*[like this]*`, is the transcriber's description of something that is not typed text: signatures, handwriting, seals, stamps and drawings. Everything else is the original's wording.
- **Nothing corrected.** Spelling, punctuation, capitalization and apparent typing errors are kept. Each file ends with transcriber's notes listing them, so a reader can tell an original error from a transcription error.
- **Line breaks.** Lines within a paragraph are rejoined, and words the typist split across lines are made whole. Paragraph breaks follow the original.
- **Headings.** Run-in headings underlined in the original are shown in **bold**. Sub-section numbers are typed as the original has them (".1", ".2"), without the section number in front.
- **Strikethrough.** `~~Text~~` marks words lined out by hand on the recorded page.
- **Blanks.** Underscores mark blanks left in the original.
- **Signatures.** A signature is named only when the instrument itself types the signer's name. Otherwise it is shown as `*[signature]*`.
- **Omitted.** The attorney's printed footer on the 1976 pages, typed page numbers, and the Clerk's book-and-page stamp (carried by the page marker instead).

## What is not transcribed

- **Drawings.** The survey, site plan, floor plans and roof plan (Exhibit "A" in 1976, 1978 and 1995) and the unit floor plans in the Prospectus are described, with their legible lettering. Dimensions, unit outlines and parking space numbers must be taken from the recorded drawings. The Clerk's note at O.R. 9640, p. 325 says the condominium plans are in Condominium Plan Book 56, Page 4.
- **The unrecorded copy.** `1977-Lenox Plaza CORPORATION-CERTIFICATE.pdf` is an earlier, unrecorded state of the Articles, By-Laws and Declaration. It was used as a second reading of the same typescript. Where it differs from the recorded pages, the recorded pages are followed and the difference is noted in file 01.
- **The Clerk's 2022 payment receipt** scanned as the first page of the Declaration Part I file.
- **The ALTERED folder.** Those are summaries, not originals.

## How the text was checked

Each page was read by OCR and then typed and corrected against the page image. The finished text was compared word by word against the OCR of every available scan of that page, and each disagreement was settled by looking at the image. Readings that stayed uncertain are listed in each file's notes. Names in handwriting, faint stamps and drawing lettering are the least certain parts.

## Points a reader should know

- **The recorded Declaration was corrected before recording.** The unrecorded copy says "1530 PENN CORP.", "PENN PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC." and "twelve (12) apartments". The recorded pages say "1033 LENOX CORP.", "LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC." and "twenty-eight apartments", and name Dade Federal Savings and Loan Association of Miami as Insurance Trustee. See the table in file 01.
- **Later amendments change earlier text.** The 1978 resolution rewrites Declaration sections 11 and 12 in part; the 1994 amendment adds rental rules. These files reproduce each instrument as recorded and do not merge them.
- **Statutory references are to Chapter 711**, the Condominium Act in force in 1976. Current Florida law is Chapter 718.
