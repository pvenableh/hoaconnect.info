AMENDMENT TO DECLARATION OF CONDOMINIUM OF LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC.

LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC., is a non-profit Florida corporation, the Declaration Of Condominium of which is recorded in Official Records Book 9640, at Page 258, of the Public Records of Dade County, Florida.

*[the book "9640" and page "258" are handwritten]*

The Declaration Of Condominium herein is amended as follows:

1\. The following rules shall control the rental of units in this condominium.

A. A Lessee shall be approved in the same manner as the approval for the purchaser of a unit.

B. There shall be $100.00 fee paid to the Association by the unit owner to defray costs and efforts in the investigation of such "approval" for a lessee.

C. No unit owner owner shall lease a unit unless it has been own by that person for not less than two (2) years.

D. As a condition precedent to the approval of a lessee, the unit owner shall post with the Association a security bond deposit of $200.00, plus six (6) months advance monthly maintenance payments. Such funds may be comingled with the general revenues of the Associaition and no interest shall be paid thereon.

E. No lease period shall be for less than one (1) year nor more than two (2) years, and there shall not be any option to renew therein.

F. Attached to the lease shall be an "addendum" as approved by the Association, which shall become a part of the lease and which shall prevail over any provisions in such lease contrary thereto.

G. The failure to comply with the foregoing provisions shall automatically void any lease agreement.

2\. There shall be no parking of vans on the premises of this condominium.

The foregoing amendments were duly approved and adopted by the required percentage of unit owners of this condominium. That meeting was properly noticed and scheduled and thereafter held in the County Of Dade, State Of Florida, on the 4th day of November, 1992.

We, the undersigned officers, do further certify that at a special meeting of the Board of Directors, in full compliance with the necessary notice thereof, held on the 4th day of November, 1992, it was duly resolved that the above amendments be ratified and approved accordingly.

This instrument is intended to comply with the requirements of Florida Statute, Section 718.110 wherein a Certificate of the Association is mandated.
