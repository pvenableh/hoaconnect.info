EXHIBIT "D"

BY-LAWS

OF

LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC.,

A CONDOMINIUM

1\. **Identity.** These are the By-Laws of LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC., herein called "Association", a non-profit Florida Corporation organized pursuant to Chapters 617 and 711, Florida Statutes for the purpose of administering LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC., a Condominium, being a Condominium of lands lying and being situate in Dade County, Florida, as more particularly set forth in its Declaration of Condominium.

.1 **Office.** The office of the Association shall be at the premises of the condominium.

.2 **Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year of the Association shall be the calendar year.

2\. **Members.**

.1 **Qualification.** The members of the Association shall consist of all of the record owners of units.

.2 **Change of Membership.** Change of membership in the Association shall be established by recording in the Public Records of Dade County, Florida, a deed or other instrument establishing record title to a unit in the condominium and the delivery to the Association of a certified copy of such instrument, the owner designated by such instrument thereby becoming a member of the Association. The membership of the prior owner shall be thereby terminated. The Association may issue certificates of membership.

.3 **Voting Rights.** There shall be assigned to each unit one vote and the member owning such unit shall be entitled to cast same.

.4 **Designation of Voting Representative.** If a unit is owned by one person his right to vote shall be established by the record title to his unit. If a unit is owned by more

{O.R. 9640, p. 299}

than one person, the person entitled to cast the vote for the unit shall be designed by a certificate signed by all of the record owners of the unit and filed with the Secretary of the Association. If a unit is owned by a corporation, trust or other non-personal entity, the person entitled to cast the vote for the unit shall be designated by a certificate of appointment signed by its chief executive officer and filed with the Secretary of the Association. Such certificate shall be valid until revoked or until superseded by a subsequent certificate or until a change in the ownership of the unit concerned. A Certificate designating the person entitled to cast the vote of a unit may be revoked by any owner thereof.

.5 **Approval or Disapproval of Matters.** Whenever the decision of a unit owner is required upon any matter, whether or not the subject of an Association meeting, such decision shall be expressed by the same person who would case the vote of such owner if at an Association meeting, unless the joinder of record owners is specifically required by the Declaration or these By-Laws.

.6 **Restraint Upon Assignment of Shares in Assets.** The share of a member in the funds and assets of the Association cannot be assigned, hypothecated or transferred in any manner except as an appurtenance to his unit.

3\. **Members' Meeting.**

.1 **Annual Members' Meeting.** The annual members' meeting shall be held at the condominium property at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Standard Time, on the first Tuesday of December of each year, commencing with the first December subsequent to the year in which the Declaration of Condominium is recorded in the Public Records of Dade County, Florida, for the purpose of electing officers and transacting any other business authorized to be transacted by the members.

{O.R. 9640, p. 300}

.2 **Special Members' Meetings.** Special members' meetings shall be held whenever called by the President, or in his absence by the Vice-President, and must be called by such officer upon the receipt of a written request from members entitled to cast fifty (50%) per cent or more of the votes of the entire membership.

.3 **Notice of All Members' Meetings.** Notice of all members' meetings stating the time and place and the objects for which such meeting is called shall be given unless waived in writing. Such notice shall be in writing and furnished to each member at his address as it appears in the books of the Association and shall be mailed, which need not be by registered or certified mail, not less than fourteen (14) days nor more than sixty (60) days prior to the date of the meeting. In addition, a copy of such notice shall be posted on the condominium property at least fourteen (14) days prior to the meeting in a conspicuous manner. Proof of such mailing and posting shall be given by affidavit of the person giving the notice. Notice of Meeting may be waived before or after meetings.

.4 **Quorum.** A quorum at members' meetings shall consist of persons entitled to cast a majority of the votes of the Association. The acts approved by a majority of those present at a meeting at which a quorum is present shall constitute the acts of the members, except where approval by a greater number of members is required by the Declaration of Condominium or these By-Laws.

.5 **Proxies.** Votes may be cast in person or by proxy. Poxies may be made by any person entitled to vote and shall be valid only for the particular meeting designated therein and must be filed with the Secretary before the appointed time of the meeting or any adjournment thereof. No one person may be designated to hold more than five (5) proxies.

{O.R. 9640, p. 301}

.6 **Adjourned Meetings.** If any meeting of members cannot be organized because a quorum has not attended, the members who are present, either in person or by proxy, may adjourn the meeting from time to time until a quorum is present.

.7 **Order of Business.** The order of business at annual members' meetings, and as far as practical at all other members' meetings, shall be:

(a) Calling of the roll and certifying of proxies.

(b) Proof of notice of meeting or waiver of notice.

(c) Reading and disposal of any unapproved minutes.

(d) Reports of officers.

(e) Election of officers.

(f) Unfinished business.

(g) New business.

(h) Adjournment.

.8 **Minutes.** Minutes of all meetings shall be kept in a businesslike manner and shall be available for inspection by all members and directors at all reasonable times.

4\. **Directors.**

.1 **Directors and Their Election.** The Association shall act through its directors. Prior to the unit owners, other than the Developer being entitled to elect a majority of the directors, the number of directors shall be as the Developer may determine provided there shall be no less than three (3). When the members shall become entitled to elect a majority of the directors, then the number of directors shall be no less than five (5) nor more than seven (7). Provided, however, that until December 31, 1976, all of the directors shall be the designees and nominees of the Developer subject nevertheless to the following: When members other than the Developer own fifteen (15%) per cent or more of the condominium units, the members other than the Developer shall be entitled to elect not less than one-third (1/3) of the members of the Board. Members other than the

{O.R. 9640, p. 302}

Developer shall be entitled to elect not less than a majority of the members of the Board three (3) years after sales by the Developer have been closed of seventy-five (75) percent of the condominium units or three (3) months after sales have been closed by the Developer of ninety (90%) percent of the condominium units, or when all of the units have been completed and some have been sold and none of the others are being offered for sale by the Developer in the ordinary course of business, whichever shall first occur. Notwithstanding anything above to the contrary, the Developer shall be entitled to elect not less than one (1) member of the Board so long as the Developer holds for sale in the ordinary course of business any unit. The Developer may at any time elect to terminate its right to control the Association through its designee-directors which election shall be affected by giving notice to all of the members. Such notice shall be in writing and furnished to each member at his address as it appears in the books of the Association. Within thirty (30) days of the furnishing of such notice a special members' meeting shall be elected by the members. Should said special members' meeting not be attended by a quorum, thereby making the election of successor directors impossible, then the designee-directors shall continue in office until their successors shall have been elected but the Developer shall be released and relieved of all further liability with regard to quaranteed assessments and installments, all as provided in Section ____ of the Declaration. Directors elected by the members may be peremptorily removed without cause by the vote of the members at any meeting and their successors thereat; but the director who is the designee of the club unit owner may only be removed and his successor may only be designated by such club unit member. Directors designated by the Developer may be removed by it at any time without cause and their successors designated by the Developer. Voting for directors by members shall be on a non-cumulative basis, each member being entitled to cast one vote for each apartment he owns

{O.R. 9640, p. 303}

for each vacancy to be filled. Directors shall serve until the next annual members' meeting or until their successor is elected. Director-designees of the Developer and the member who is the club unit owner need not be members; all other directors shall be members or the voting representative of a corporation, trust or other non-personal entity which is a member.

.2 **Vacancies.** If a vacancy in the position of Director shall come about by reason of his removal, death, resignation, incapacity or otherwise, such vacancy shall be filled by the remaining directors, except as provided in 4.1.

.3 **Organization Meeting.** The organization meeting of a newly elected Board of Directors shall be held within ten (10) days of their election at such place and time as shall be fixed by the directors at the meeting at which they were elected, and no further notice of organization meeting shall be necessary.

.4 **Regular Meetings.** Regular meetings of the Board of Directors may be held at such time and place as shall be determined from time to time by a majority of the directors. Notice of regular meetings shall be given to each director, personally or by mail, telephone or telegraph, at least three (3) days prior to the day named for such meeting.

.5 **Special Meetings.** Special meetings of the directors may be called by the President and must be called by the Secretary at the written request of a director. Not less than three (3) days' notice of the meeting shall be given personally or by mail, telephone or telegraph, which notice shall state the time, place and purpose of the meeting.

.6 **Waiver of Notice.** Any director may waive notice of a meeting before or after the meeting and such waiver shall be deemed equivalent to the giving of notice.

.7 **Quorum.** A quorum at directors' meetings shall consist of a majority of the entire Board of Directors. The acts approved by a majority of these present at a meeting at

{O.R. 9640, p. 304}

which a quorum is present shall constitute the acts of the Board of Directors.

.8 **Adjourned Meetings.** If at any meeting of the Board of Directors there be less than a quorum present, the majority of those present may adjourn the meeting from time to time until a quorum is present.

.9 **Presiding Officer.** The presiding officer of directors' meetings shall be the President. In the absence of the President the directors present shall designate one of their number to preside.

.10 **Powers and Duties.** The powers and duties of the Association under its Articles of Incorporation, these By-Laws, and under the laws of the State of Florida including but not limited to Chapters 617 and 711, Florida Statutes, are to be exercised solely and exclusively by the Board of Directors, its agents and representatives, subject to the approval of its members only when so specifically provided in said Articles of Incorporation, these By-Laws or the Declaration of Condominium.

.11 **Notice to Members and Request to Attend.** All meetings of directors shall be open to all members and notices of meetings shall be posted conspicuously at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance for the attention of members except in an emergency not permitting the same.

.12 **Minutes.** Minutes of all meetings shall be kept in a businesslike manner and shall be available for inspection by all members and directors at all reasonable times.

5\. **Officers.**

.1 **Election.** The executive officers of the Association shall be a President, a Treasurer and a Secretary, all of whom must be directors, and shall be elected annually by the directors and who may be peremptorily removed, without cause, by vote of the directors at any meeting. The Board of Directors shall fill vacancies caused by the removal or resignation of officers and shall from time to time elect such other officers and designate their powers and duties

{O.R. 9640, p. 305}

as the Board shall find to be required to manage the affairs of the Association.

.2 **President.** The President shall be the chief executive officer of the Association. He shall have all of the powers and duties which are usually vested in the office of the president of an association. He shall serve as chairman of all members' meetings.

.3 **Vice-President.** A Vice-President shall in the absence or disability of the President exercise the powers and perform the duties of the President. He shall also generally assist the President and exercise such other powers and perform such other duties as shall be prescribed by the members.

.4 **Secretary.** The Secretary shall keep the minutes of all proceedings of the officers and the members. He shall attend to the giving and serving of all notices to the members and other notices required by law. He shall keep the records of the Association, except those of the Treasurer, and shall perform all other duties incident to the office of secretary of an association and as may be required by the members or the President.

.5 **Treasurer.** The Treasurer shall have custody of all property of the Association, including funds, securities and evidences of indebtedness. He shall keep the books of the Association in accordance with good accounting practices and he shall perform all other duties incident to the office of Treasurer.

.6 **Compensation.** The compensation, if any, of all officers shall be fixed by the members at their annual meeting. No officer who is a designee of the Developer shall receive any compensation for his services as such.

6\. **Fiscal Management.** The provisions for fiscal management of the Association set forth in the Declaration of Condominium shall be supplemented by the following provisions:

.1 **Accounts.** The funds and expenditures of the

{O.R. 9640, p. 306}

Association shall be credited and charged to accounts under the following classifications as shall be appropriate:

(a) **Current Expenses.** Current expenses shall include all funds and expenditures to be made within the year for which the funds are budgeted and may include a reasonable allowance for contingencies and working funds. The balance in this fund at the end of each year may be applied to reduce the assessments for current expense for the succeeding year, or to fund reserves, or be returned to the members.

(b) **Reserve for Deferred Maintenance.** Reserve for deferred maintenance shall include funds for maintenance items which occur less frequently than annually. There shall be no requirement to establish or fund such reserve.

(c) **Reserve for Replacement.** Reserve for replacement shall include funds for reapir or replacement required because of damage, depreciation or obsolescence. There shall be no requirement to establish or fund such reserve.

.2 **Budget.** No later than November 1 of each year, commencing with the year in which the certificate of occupancy is issued as to the condominium property (provided at least four (4) months shall have transpired between the date of issuance of such certificate and the next succeeding November 1), the directors shall prepare a proposed budget of common expense for the ensuing calendar year and shall mail a copy of such proposed budget to all members not less than thirty (30) days prior to a meeting of the directors at which the budget will be considered. Until the first budget shall be adopted in accordance with the foregoing provisions, that certain proposed budget being a part of the Developer's disclosure documents of this condominium shall be in effect. Together with such proposed budget, the members shall be given written notice of the time and place of such meeting

{O.R. 9640, p. 307}

and such meeting shall be open to all members. If the Board shall adopt a budget which requires assessments against the members exceeding 115% of the assessments for the preceding year, then upon written application of ten (10%) per cent or more of the members a special members' meeting shall be held not less than ten (10) days prior to written notice to each member but within thirty (30) days of the delivery of such application to the Board or any member thereof. At such special members' meeting the members may consider and enact a revision of the budget or recall any or all members of the Board and elect their successors. The revision of the budget or recall of any or all members of the Board shall require a vote of not less than a majority of the whole number of votes of all members. The Board may, in any event, propose a budget to the members at a meeting of members or by writing, and if such budget or proposed budget be approved by the unit owners at the meeting or by a majority of their whole number in writing, such budget shall thereafter be reestimated by the members in the manner hereinabove set forth nor shall directors be recalled under the terms of this section. In determining whether assessments exceed 115% of similar assessments in prior years, there shall be excluded in the computation any provision for reasonable reserves made by the directors in respect of repair or replacement of the common property or in respect of anticipated expenses by the Association which are not anticipated to be incurred on a regular or annual basis; provided, that so long as the Developer is in control of the Board of Directors, the Board of Directors shall not impose an assessment for a year greater than 115% of the prior calendar year's assessment without approval of the majority of the members.

.3 **Assessments.** The budget and assessments thereunder shall be sufficient to produce no less funds that are required in advance for the payment of all anticipated current operating expenses and for all of the unpaid operating expenses previously incurred. Assessments against the members

{O.R. 9640, p. 308}

for their shares of the items of the budget shall be made for the calendar year annually in advance on or before December 15 preceding the year fro which the assessments are made. Such assessments shall be due in twelve (12) monthly payments, one of which shall be come due on the first day of each month of the year for which the assessments are made. If an annual assessment is not made as required, an assessment shall be presumed to have been made in the amount of the last prior assessment and monthly payments thereon shall be due upon the first day of each month until changed by an amended assessment. In the event the annual assessment proves to be insufficient, the budget and assessments therefor may be amended at any time by the directors, subject, nevertheless to the provisions of 6.2 hereof relative to members and their rights of approval and recall as above specified and limited. The unpaid assessment for the remaining portion of the calendar year for which the amended assessment is made shall be due on the first day of the first month next succeeding the month in which such amended assessment is made or as otherwise provided by the directors.

.4 **Depository.** The depository of the Association will be such banks and/or savings and loan associations in Dade County, Florida, as shall be designated from time to time by the directors and in which the monies of the Association shall be deposited. Withdrawals of monies from such accounts shall only be by checks signed by such persons as authorized by the directors.

.5 **Fidelity Bonds.** Fidelity bonds may be required by the directors from all persons handling or responsible for Association funds. The amount of such bonds shall be determined by the directors. The premiums on such bonds shall be paid by the Association.

{O.R. 9640, p. 309}

.6 **Initial Contributions.** Each of the Developer's immediate grantees (other than the Developer itself, its nominee or a successor or alternate developer) as to a unit, at the time of cloring upon his unit shall make an initial contribution to the Association equal to two (2) months maintenance, which contribution shall not be applicable to any future assessments or installments relative to such unit. Such contributions may be used by the Association for any of its purposes, including current common expense, prepayments, purchase of equipment and other Association purposes, and the same need not be segregated or reserved.

.7 **Accounting Records.** The Association shall maintain accounting records according to good accounting practices which shall be open to inspection by members or their authorized representatives at reasonable times, and written summaries of which shall be supplied at least annually to the members or their authorized representatives. Failure of the Association to permit inspection of its accounting records by unit owners or their authorized representatives shall entitle any person prevailing in an action for enforcement to recover reasonable attorneys' fees from the Association. Such records shall include all of those items set forth in Section 711.12(7) of the Florida Statutes.

7\. **Parliamentary Rules.** Roberts' Rules of Order (latest edition) shall govern the conduct of Association meetings when not in conflict with the Declaration of Condominium or these By-Laws.

8\. **Developer.** Notwithstanding any other provisions of these By-Laws, so long as the Developer holds units for sale in the ordinary course of business, neither the Developer nor such units may be assessed directly or indirectly for any capital improvements nor may the Association take any action of any nature or description which would be detrimental to the

{O.R. 9640, p. 310}

sale of such unsold units by the Developer.

9\. **Amendment.** The By-Laws may be amended only in the manner set forth in the Articles of Incorporation.

LENOX PLAZA ASSOCIATION, INC.,

a Condominium,

## Transcriber's notes

**Wording kept as it appears in the original**

- 2.4: "shall be designed by a certificate". 2.5: "would case the vote". 3.5: "Poxies". 4.7: "a majority of these present". 6.1(c): "reapir". 6.3: "the year fro which", "shall be come due", "no less funds that are required". 6.6: "cloring". 7: "Roberts' Rules of Order".  
- 4.1: "a special members' meeting shall be elected by the members"; "shall be affected by giving notice"; "quaranteed"; "seventy-five (75) percent" without the % sign; the section number in "all as provided in Section ____ of the Declaration" is left blank; "their successors thereat" has no verb. The section also refers to a "club unit owner" and "club unit member".  
- 5.1 requires the President, Treasurer and Secretary all to be directors. Article 9 of the Articles says "no other officer need be a Director".  
- 6.2: "not less than ten (10) days prior to written notice"; and "such budget shall thereafter be reestimated by the members … nor shall directors be recalled", where a negative appears to be missing before "thereafter".  
- 9 says the By-Laws may be amended only as set forth in the Articles. Article 13 provides for altering the By-Laws by a majority vote of the membership. Article 15 sets the 21-unit vote for amending the Articles themselves.

**Readings settled by the transcriber**

- Sub-section numbers are typed as in the original (".1", ".2"), which omits the section number. Run-in headings shown in bold are underlined in the original.  
- 3.1 ("recorded in"), 4.1 ("each apartment he owns") and 4.11 ("in an emergency"): the last letter of the line is clipped at the right margin of the scans and has been supplied.  
- 6.2: a stray mark follows "prepare"; omitted.  
- The attorney's printed footer on each page is omitted.  
- The recorded and unrecorded scans were compared word for word and no difference in wording was found.
